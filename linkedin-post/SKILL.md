# LinkedIn Post Writing Skill

## Who This Is For

**Profile:** Alara Akcasiz — AI PMM & Growth, ~5K followers, personal profile (not company page)
**Audience:** Primarily Turkish professionals, but content is written in English with simple, universally accessible language
**Voice:** Conversational + analytical + a little fun. Born 1997 — not cringe millennial, not Gen Z either. Self-aware, slightly bold, never corporate.
**Style reference:** HBR tone — business insight through personal experience. Not hardcore marketing speak. Simple words, everyday business scenarios, consumable for non-native English speakers.

---

## The Non-Negotiables (Never Break These)

1. **No external links in the post body** — links drop impressions 27% on personal profiles. Always put links in the first comment.
2. **Always end with a direct question** — posts with questions get 77% more comments.
3. **2–5 hashtags at the end** — broad enough to reach beyond existing audience, relevant to topic. Never more than 5.
4. **Never edit after posting** — edited posts get suppressed by LinkedIn algorithm.
5. **Add a personal photo** — Alara's photo posts consistently outperform text-only. Suggest a relevant selfie or candid work photo.
6. **Short paragraphs** — one idea per line. Written to be scanned, not read.

## Post Structure

**The Formula That Works:**
```
Hook (stops the scroll)
↓
Personal moment or counterintuitive insight
↓
Simple framework, observation, or lesson
↓
Closing line that lands
↓
Direct question to audience
↓
[blank line]
#Hashtag1 #Hashtag2 #Hashtag3
```

## Writing the Hook

The first 2 lines are everything — they appear before "see more."

**Rules:**
- Never start with "I"
- Start with the tension, the stat, or the idea
- Must create curiosity or mild discomfort
- Counterintuitive > obvious

**Hook formulas that work for Alara:**
- Bold contrarian statement: *"The AI presentation market hit $1.94B this year. And most of it is producing garbage."*
- Personal confession: *"I have a slightly embarrassing confession. I build AI products. I talk about AI every day."*
- Surprising number: *"88% of employees use AI at work. Only 5% use it in ways that actually transform results."*
- Reframe: *"Most brands obsess over the first sale. I used to do the same."*

## Content Types & How to Handle Each

### Repurposing an Article or Case Study
- Extract the single most counterintuitive insight — that becomes the hook
- Turn data points into punchy standalone lines
- Never summarize the article — tease it, make them want to click
- Put the link in the first comment, not the post
- End with: "Full breakdown in the comments 👇"

### Original Thought Leadership
- Start with a personal moment or observation from real work
- Build to a business insight
- Keep the framework simple: 3 points max, or a clear before/after
- Never use marketing jargon — write like you're explaining to a smart friend

### List Posts (5 things, X habits, etc.)
- Hook must frame WHY the list matters, not just announce it
- Each item gets 2–4 lines max — the insight + the practical application
- Bold the category name, plain text for the explanation
- End the list with a closing line that ties it together, then the question

### Repurposing Bad-Performing Posts
- Identify why it underperformed: usually external link, no question, or wrong format
- Rewrite as pure text with personal hook
- Add question at end
- Suggest pairing with personal photo

## Timing Guidance (Always Mention When Relevant)

- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best time:** 8:00–10:00am local time (8:30am has worked best for Alara)
- **Avoid:** Evenings, weekends, posting past 9pm
- **Why it matters:** Half of a post's lifetime impressions happen in first 48 hours. Wrong timing = wasted content.
- **Pro tip:** Schedule in advance rather than posting live at off-hours

---

## What Kills Reach (Flag These If You See Them)

- External link in post body
- No question at the end
- Posting at night or on weekends
- Editing after publishing
- Carousels that feel like branded decks (not personal stories)
- Writing like a marketer instead of a thinking professional
- Too many hashtags (6+)
- Generic opener ("In today's fast-paced world...")

## Hashtag Selection Guide

**Always pick 3–5 from these categories:**

| Topic | Good hashtags |
|-------|--------------|
| AI & Tech | #AI #AITools #ArtificialIntelligence #AIStrategy |
| Marketing | #Marketing #GrowthMarketing #MarketingStrategy #B2BMarketing |
| Productivity | #Productivity #FutureOfWork #WorkSmarter |
| Professional growth | #ProfessionalDevelopment #CareerGrowth #Leadership |
| Product | #ProductManagement #SaaS #ProductMarketing |
| Presentations | #Presentations #PublicSpeaking |

**Rule:** Pick hashtags that match the post topic, not Alara's job title. Broader = more reach.

## Tone Checklist (Before Finalizing Any Post)

- [ ] Does it sound like a real person, not a brand?
- [ ] Would a non-marketer understand every sentence?
- [ ] Is there a real point of view, not just information?
- [ ] Does the hook create curiosity before "see more"?
- [ ] Is the question at the end genuine, not rhetorical filler?
- [ ] Are there 2–5 hashtags?
- [ ] Is there a photo suggestion?
- [ ] Is the link in the comments, not the post?
